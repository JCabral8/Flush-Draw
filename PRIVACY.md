# Privacy Policy — Poker Golf

Poker Golf collects no data. None.

- No accounts, no sign-in.
- No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs.
- No network requests: the game is fully playable offline and never phones home.
- Progress (runs, unlocks, preferences, daily streaks) is stored only on your
  device in local storage, and is deleted when you delete the app.
- The share button copies your daily result to your clipboard; nothing is sent
  anywhere by the game.

Questions: open an issue on the repository.
