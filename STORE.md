## Description

Your deck is your golf bag. Every swing is a poker hand: pairs are your
workhorse irons, straights are your long game, and a royal flush flies five
hundred yards — which is exactly why you should almost never play one.

Poker Golf punishes long, not short. The hole doesn't want your biggest
hand; it wants the *right* hand, and the 2s and 3s you dumped on the front
nine are the putts you'll miss on the back.

- 27-hole tour runs with cut lines that end soft rounds early
- 40 caddies who bend the rules — one of them never lets a putt roll past
- 24 clubs; the five you carry are the build
- A 52-card deck that must survive nine holes: the reshuffle costs a stroke
- Daily course: one attempt, same deal as the whole world
- Match play against the Club Pro's recorded round
- Fully offline. No ads, no energy, no loot boxes, ever.

Fifteen minutes a run. Weeks to stop thinking about it.
